The Webbo3 Non-Negotiables We Held

  • Zero AI Gradients: Strictly flat, chosen colors inspired by our mother-and-child milestone identity mark.
  • No Generic "Hero + 3 Cards": Every single layout was crafted specifically for its public health purpose.
  • One Shared Identity: Every page imports css/brand.css before its individual stylesheet.
  • Zero Lorem Ipsum: Every disease, vaccine code, clinic time, and transport estimate is grounded in Nigerian reality.
  • Pure HTML and CSS: No React, no Tailwind, no external JavaScript. Accessible, lightweight, and blazingly fast.
