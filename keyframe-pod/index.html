Crucial Caregiver Fact

Did you miss an immunisation date? You do not have to restart.

Many mothers stay away because they fear nurses will scold them or believe the entire cycle was wasted. That is false. The vaccines your child already took are still working. Pick up at your nearest Primary Health Centre on their next routine clinic day.

Beware the Drop-off Trap

The Danger Zone: Between 14 Weeks and 9 Months

After your baby's 14-week appointment, there is a 5-month break before the 9-month Measles and Yellow Fever visit. Over 30% of Nigerian children drop out of routine immunisation during this silence because cards get lost or life gets busy. Set a calendar reminder now.
