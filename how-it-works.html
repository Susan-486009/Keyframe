The Caregiver Workflow

Four simple steps to keep your baby protected

KeyFrame is built to work seamlessly alongside your physical Child Health Card. We never ask you to create a complicated account or download an app. Here is how a mother or father uses this site in daily life.

Step 01

Check Your Child's Age or Last Visit

Open KeyFrame from any phone browser. Look at your child's age (e.g. 6 weeks, 10 weeks, or 9 months) on our milestone timeline to immediately identify what comes next.

See timeline →
Step 02

Read What The Injection Prevents

Instead of confusing medical codes like "Penta 1" or "PCV 2", read our plain-language explanations of illnesses stopped (severe pneumonia, whooping cough, lockjaw, and diarrhea).

Read 6-week guide →
Step 03

Pack Your Bag & Prepare Small Cash

Before setting off for the clinic morning, use our clinic checklist. Pack two clean baby wrappers, spare baby clothes, wipes, your paper yellow card, and small change for transport.

Open packing list →
Step 04

Use Catch-Up Guidance if Delayed

If weeks or months passed since your scheduled date, consult our honest catch-up protocol. You will know exactly what to say to the triage nurse without feeling ashamed or afraid.

Read catch-up script →
