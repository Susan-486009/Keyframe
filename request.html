Best Time to Arrive

Arrive by 7:30 AM to 8:00 AM. Most PHCs close registration by 10:30 AM so nurses can batch-open 10-dose and 20-dose vaccine vials (like BCG and Measles) before midday heat.

Dress Baby in Loose, Snap-Button Clothing

At 6, 10, and 14 weeks, injections are given in the thighs. Loose clothes make undressing fast and reduce crying on the table.

Lost Your Yellow Card?

Go to clinic anyway! Do not miss an appointment because a card was soaked or lost. Report to the record desk for an official replacement.
